About

I trained as a software engineer. For my degree I worked on a web tool for comparing long genome sequences, and I’ve been drawn to the place where code meets biology ever since.

My day job is mobile: native iOS in Swift, Android in Kotlin and cross-platform apps in Flutter, shipped to real users. I care about clean architecture (MVVM), tested logic and tidy interfaces. In bioinformatics I care about really understanding the data (transcripts, population frequencies, ClinVar), not just the “most severe consequence”.
